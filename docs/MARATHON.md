The engine locks the entire piece, removes all completed rows, collapses the board and adds 100/300/500/800 times the **pre-clear** level. It retains all cleared lines, including overshoot. If the new count is at least 300, status becomes `won`, active becomes null and gravity elapsed becomes zero **before** any next spawn/refill. Queue, RNG and next type remain unchanged. `won` is inert under Start, movement, rotation, drops, gravity and pause. New game/Reset creates a new level-1 game; controller Reset also advances the request epoch and rejects stale replies.

Blocked spawn remains `over` with reason `blocked spawn`. The controller/facades preserve `won` separately from loss/error. Caps stay censored, never victories. No competitive winner is inferred.
